WE ARE IN TOWN

HENGE MIAMI

Henge is proud to announce the grand opening of its first flagship store in the United States, located in the heart of Miami's Design District.
The 350-square-metre space celebrates Henge's distinctive aesthetic and design philosophy, where unique materials and timeless craftsmanship blend seamlessly with contemporary, visionary design.
The showroom is conceived as a true “design atelier”, offering architects, designers, and design enthusiasts an immersive experience into the world of Henge.

DISCOVER

The collections on display include iconic pieces alongside new releases, presented within an interior concept defined by striking contrasts and a masterful balance of materials. Every piece is handcrafted in Italy using carefully selected materials and exclusive artisanal techniques.
The opening of this flagship store represents a significant step in Henge's international growth, as it continues to bring its vision of luxury and design to a
global audience.

MILAN FASHION WEEK
2025

Henge proudly unveils its highly anticipated 2025 Collection at Milan Design Week.
The new collection embodies Henge's signature approach to materiality and craftsmanship, featuring exclusive collaborations with renowned designers Tanju Özelgin and Johanna Grawunder, both of whom are collaborating with Henge for the first time this year, alongside Yabu Pushelberg, with whom Henge has previously worked.
Additionally, the collection showcases pieces from Henge's internal team and regular collaborators, including Massimo Castagna, Ugo Cacciatori, and Isabella Genovese.

READ MORE

Beyond the showroom, Henge also debuts a sophisticated kitchen concept designed by Patricia Urquiola for Alchemica, this year’s show curated by Elle Decor Italia at Palazzo Bovara. Inspired by the transformation of matter, this “kitchen-laboratory” captures the essence of alchemy through a stunning material composition.
